Welcome to the Murfreesboro Regional Soccer League

Part of the North American Recreational Soccer Association

The MRSL is open to players of all levels who want to play soccer in a relax friendly environment.
Even if they never played soccer before the MRSL is a place to start.

To get a feel for our league, we recommend you call us at the number below to stop by Davies
Sporting Goods to talk to one of our coordinators and get the details on our next all-team practice or workshop day.
Then come kick around the ball with us and meet other players in the league

If you're interested in joining up, you will need to complete some paperwork and pay $65 for a
seasonal membership($40 for seniors and students)

We look forward to seeing you on the field
